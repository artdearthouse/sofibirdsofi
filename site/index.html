    “the birds are seeking the sun, carrying flowers and songs”✨

    This phrase is like a little title for everything I make. The birds came from a song, and the idea that a world without flowers and songs would slowly lose its mind came from a story that has stayed with me.
    To me, “flowers and songs” are the small joyful things that keep the earth alive: creativity, love, gentleness, and the things we make for each other. They are what protect the world from going mad.
    The birds searching for the sun are people like me — travelers looking for their own light, their own home, and the sun inside themselves. And the flowers and songs are what I try to give to the world along the way.
    I hope this bag carries a little of that with you too💛

    Please note: the phrase is written in my native language, because it is a story about me. But if you’d like, I can embroider a different phrase for you — your own little reminder to keep close
